===================================================================
  HOW TO ADD YOUR IMAGES AND VIDEOS TO THIS PORTFOLIO
===================================================================

Welcome! You can easily showcase your own images, screenshots,
artwork, photography, and video demos in the Media Gallery page:

METHOD 1: Drop Files into this Folder (`assets/media/`)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Copy your image files (.png, .jpg, .jpeg, .webp, .gif, .svg) 
   and video files (.mp4, .webm, .ogg) into this folder:
   `c:\Users\Irene\OneDrive\Documents\PORTFOLIO\assets\media\`

2. Open `assets/js/app.js` and add your items to the `INITIAL_MEDIA` 
   array, or if running with PHP (`php -S localhost:8000`), the 
   built-in PHP API at `api/media.php` can automatically detect them!

METHOD 2: Instant Browser Drag & Drop / File Selector
-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the portfolio in your browser (`index.html` or `index.php`).
2. Go to the "Media & Video Gallery" tab.
3. Click the "Add Your Media" button at the top of the gallery.
4. Select or drag & drop any image or video file from your computer!
5. It will immediately show up in the gallery with instant live 
   preview, playback, and lightbox without needing to touch any code!

Enjoy building your portfolio!
